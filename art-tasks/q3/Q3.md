# Q3: creature pictures for the swap (374 creatures)

The Library shows the book picture from 5etools first, with a small painted thumbnail in the corner that swaps to our own picture (owner: "aku suka yang kombinasi 5etools dan art codex"). 374 creatures of the 2024 Monster Manual still have no picture of ours, so the swap button is missing on them. Draw them in the same style as the 170 already in the app.

## Rules

THREE TESTS FOR EVERY PICTURE (Claude rejects any picture that fails one): 1. ART STYLE: matches the style reference exactly. 2. CONTEXT: shows exactly the subject of its line (LOOK and POSE), recognisable at a glance. 3. UNIQUE: it does not repeat the pose, layout or base object of other pictures, the set must look varied, never uniform.

1. Work only on branch art-tasks. Never touch main.
2. CLAIM, NO LANES. Any number of sessions can run at once. Take the first batch in the Status table whose status cell is empty: write "claimed by <your session id> <UTC time>" in it, commit, git pull --rebase, push at once. If the push conflicts, pull and take the next empty batch. Never work on a batch someone else claimed. A claim older than 3 hours with no pushed picture is stale: you may take it over (write your own claim over it).
3. ONE image per creature: 1024x1024 PNG, transparent background. The whole creature in view, centered, about 80% of the canvas, nothing cut at the edges, no ground, no scenery, no frame.
4. STYLE LOCK. Attach art-tasks/q3/style-ref-creature.png (16 approved creature pictures from the app) to EVERY generation, not only the first. Generate each picture as a new member of that exact set, never from memory and never "improved". Hold every point of the Style block in the prompt. Do not change the prompt text except the CREATURE line.
4b. CONTEXT FIRST. Creatures of one family (the ten dragon colours, the giants, the devils) get their own signature head, horns, body shape and pose, never the same drawing in a new colour. A player must recognise the creature at a glance: show its signature body parts, gear and role from the creature line (a bandit captain with a cutlass and captain's coat, a cultist in a hooded robe with a ritual dagger, a dragon wyrmling small and young, an adult dragon big with the right colour and horns). Several creatures of one family (cultist, cultist fanatic, cult hierophant) must look clearly different in rank: plainer gear for the low rank, richer gear and magic for the high rank.
4d. ONE PICTURE, ONE POSE. Every creature line has its own POSE written by Claude, and no two creatures share the same POSE. Draw exactly that action, camera angle and arm position. BANNED in every picture: one open hand or claw reaching out toward the viewer, and the front-facing wide-leg stance. Claude compares each new picture with every picture already drawn, and any repeat of a pose or of the reaching hand is a redo.
4e. CORRECT ANATOMY. Head, chest, hips and feet must face directions a real body can take: never a face turned toward the viewer on a body seen from behind, no twisted neck, no reversed knees on humanoids, the right number of arms, legs, fingers and heads. Broken anatomy is a redo.
4c. LOOK FIRST. Every creature line starts with LOOK: written by Claude. Draw exactly that: the body, the signature features, the gear and the pose it names. INFO is background for details. A picture that misses a LOOK feature is redone.
5. NO WAITING. There is no trial gate any more. Keep working batch after batch until no empty batch is left. Before each new batch: git pull --rebase, and first redo every name marked "redo" in rows you or anyone worked on and nobody has claimed for redo (write "redo claimed by <id>" in that row while you work on it). When the redo is pushed, write "redo done, waiting" in that row.
6. Draw the creature from its D&D description in your own design. Never copy official D&D art, 5etools pictures or tokens. A different pose from the book picture is welcome, since the two pictures sit side by side.
7. Save art-tasks/q3-out/<slug>.png (slug from the list). After a batch: commit "q3: B0NN", git pull --rebase, push.
8. Claude checks every pushed batch for style and for every LOOK feature, and writes "redo" with the reason in the Status row. Approval of the trial is not approval of later batches.
9. Claude checks every pushed picture. Do not run your own quality checks or scripts on the pictures. Just generate, save and push.

## Prompt per creature

```
Make ONE image: 1024x1024 PNG, transparent background. The whole creature, centered, about 80% of the canvas, nothing cut off, no ground, no scenery, no frame, no shadow plate.
CREATURE: <the LOOK text of the creature line>. POSE: <the POSE text>, never the stock front-facing wide stance. (Info for details only: <the INFO text>.)
Style: a new member of the attached style-ref-creature.png set (16 approved app pictures), indistinguishable in technique:
- Western fantasy comic-book illustration, full body, three-quarter view, a confident action pose.
- Crisp dark ink linework, medium weight, with fine hatching lines inside the shadows and on fur, cloth and scales.
- Rich saturated painted colour with clear cel-shaded light and shadow planes, warm key light from the upper left, a cool blue or teal rim light on the shadow side.
- High detail on materials: fur strands, feathers, scales, cloth folds, worn metal with scratches.
- Natural proportions (not chibi, not cute, not anime eyes), adults only.
NOT allowed: soft airbrush or painterly blur, watercolor, pastel or washed-out colour, flat vector shapes, 3D render or plastic look, photorealism, anime or Ghibli look, glow haze, thick cartoon outlines, neon colours, scenery, text, letters, runes, logos, watermarks, gore.
Your own design from the D&D description. Never copy official D&D art, Monster Manual art, 5etools pictures or tokens.
```
