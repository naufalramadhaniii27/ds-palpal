# What's New entries before the public numbering (v18 to v102)

These entries have no label, so the app never showed them. They moved out of the shell file to cut its size (fonts_dist budget). Nothing here is read by code.

## v102 (2026-10-26)
- New Situations card in Actions. It lists only the conditional features your character has: Reckless Attack, Sneak Attack, Brutal Strike, Sunlight Sensitivity, Pack Tactics, Hex and more. Turn one on and the Advantage, Disadvantage, AC or Speed change shows on the numbers. Per-turn items have a Used box that clears on New turn or a rest.
- Magic ammunition (+1 to +3) adds to hit and damage. With more than one kind, pick the active one in the attack details.
- Items that give darkvision now show it in Senses.
- Skulker gives Blindsight 10 ft, Boon of Communication gives Telepathy 120 ft, and the Bear Aspect of the Totem Warrior doubles carrying capacity.
- Scout Ambush Master and Pallid Elf Incisive Sense now show their Advantage tags.

## v101 (2026-10-26)
- Fixed: a long spell text in the builder (Alter Self) cut off the top and could not scroll. It scrolls now.
- Draconic Ancestry now shows what your dragon type does: Breath Weapon shape, save and damage type, and the matching Damage Resistance.
- Wild Shape in Creatures tells you when you have not picked your known forms yet. On the 2014 rules it explains that there is no known-forms list.
- Creatures has a search box for long lists.
- Spells cast on you: the Hit Point maximum menu now has Aid (by slot level), Heroes' Feast (2d10) and Harm. One tap changes your maximum and current Hit Points. The Aid, Heroes' Feast and Harm spell cards link to it.
- New Library: a floating mini window that searches every spell, magic item, piece of equipment, feat, species, background, class, subclass, feature, class option, creature and rule. Press L or open More, Library. Tap a result to read it in the same window. On a phone it opens as a bottom sheet.
- Library search forgives typos, in names and in description text (fierball finds Fireball).
- Library Rules now hold the full 2024 Rules Glossary: 96 more entries such as Cover, Critical Hit, Area of Effect, Damage Types, Jumping and Teleportation.

## v100 (2026-10-25)
- Create and Build buttons show a progress overlay right away, so a tap never looks ignored.
- New subclass: Path of the Giant (Barbarian).
- 7 new spells and 5 new species from Volo's Guide and Wild Beyond the Witchlight, plus 4 Half-Elf variants from the Sword Coast guide.
- 39 new magic items, including the Awakened and Exalted forms and the Reliquary. Passive effects reach the sheet by themselves.
- Fixed: tapping Create or Build while the page was still loading could leave the builder without part of its data. It now waits for the page to finish.
- Hide of the Feral Guardian and Verminshroud now list their Polymorph forms in Creatures, with full stat blocks: Giant Owl, Cave Bear, Guardian Wolf, Giant Rat, Rat, Giant Wasp and Giant Scorpion.

## v99 (2026-10-25)
- Fixed: on the 2014 rules, choosing Dragonborn, Dwarf, Halfling, Half-Orc or Tiefling in the Race step did nothing. Every race opens its page now.
- Quick Build now follows the rules edition. On the 2014 rules it builds a 2014 character with a 2014 race and background.
- On the 2014 rules the race lists show each race once, without the 2024 twin.
- On the 2014 rules the Extras tab no longer shows an empty bar where the Bastion would be.

## v98 (2026-10-25)

On the 2024 rules, All 2024 books turns on every 2024 source, and All WotC books turns on every Wizards of the Coast source, 2024 and 2014 together. Turn all off clears them. Homebrew sources are never touched.
