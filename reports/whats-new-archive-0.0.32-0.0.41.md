

# Archived What's New entries 0.0.32 to 0.0.41 (moved out of the shell file to keep index.html under 2 MB)

## 0.0.41 (2026-11-16)
- The charges tracker of 78 staves, wands and similar items now shows the roll you make when the last charge is spent.
- Black Flag Vanguard class with the Marshal subclass, 14 stratagems and the War Banner. Mechanist hit die corrected to d10.

## 0.0.40 (2026-11-16)
- Black Flag Mechanist class with the Metallurgist subclass and 13 Augment effects, from the Kobold Press reference document. Source BFR is off until you turn it on.

## 0.0.39 (2026-11-16)
- Kobold Press Compilation (16 spells) and Warlock Zine (26 spells), 2014 rules, via Open5e, with the class lists the sources print. Sources KPC and WZ are off until you turn them on.

## 0.0.38 (2026-11-16)
- Deep Magic Extended (Kobold Press, 2014 rules, via Open5e): 15 spells that the Deep Magic import did not have. Source KPDMX is off until you turn it on.

## 0.0.37 (2026-11-16)
- Spells That Don't Suck (somanyrobots, 2014 rules, via Open5e): 180 spells with full class lists. Source STDS is off until you turn it on.

## 0.0.36 (2026-11-16)
- Tome of Heroes (Kobold Press, 2014 rules): 11 species and 40 subraces, 19 backgrounds, 15 feats, 140 spells, 50 magic items with 91 gear and 35 weapon texts, Metamagic and Eldritch Invocations, 42 draconic runes and the Gunfighting style. Monsters and variant rules stay reference only.

## 0.0.35 (2026-11-16)
- Healer and Summoner classes (2024 rules, Spaghetti0): the spell step now offers the core spells on each class list, not only the spells from their own book.

## 0.0.34 (2026-11-16)
- Arcane Archer (Unearthed Arcana 2025 Arcane Updates): the level 15 feature is now Arcane Burst, which pushes creatures away when you use Indomitable. It replaces Indomitable Teleport.

## 0.0.33 (2026-11-15)
- Picking a class, species or background in the builder is fast again, also with many sources turned on. The Library search index is now built while the page is idle, not during your click.

## 0.0.32 (2026-11-14)
- Adamantine Armor (and other magic armor or weapons with a base item): the Base item picker now stays after you choose, so you can pick Half Plate Armor, Plate Armor and the rest. The armor then gives its base AC plus Dex (capped for medium armor) once applied to the sheet.
