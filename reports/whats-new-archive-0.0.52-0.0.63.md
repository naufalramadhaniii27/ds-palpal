# Archived What's New entries 0.0.52 to 0.0.63 (moved out of the shell file to keep index.html under 2 MB)

## 0.0.63 (2026-11-18)
- Druid: Wild Resurgence and Archdruid now trade Wild Shape uses and spell slots on the Wild Shape card. Spend a slot to regain a use, or spend uses to make a slot that a Long Rest removes.

## 0.0.62 (2026-11-18)
- Font of Magic now trades slots and points on its card: spend a slot to gain points, or spend points to make a slot of level 1 to 5. Made slots vanish after a Long Rest. Font of Inspiration lets a Bard spend a slot to recover a Bardic Inspiration use.
- Ten more subclass features give their free spell with the right spellcasting ability.

## 0.0.61 (2026-11-18)
- Fourteen subclass features now give their damage resistances (some on a switch, such as Raging or Starry Form). Cleric domains with Potent Spellcasting add the Wisdom modifier to cantrip damage.
- Book of Exalted Deeds, Armor of Invulnerability, Stormgirdle and Gauntlets of Rage give their resistances and immunities. Dwarf Stonecunning, Naga Speed Burst, Tabaxi Feline Agility, Spring Mandrake and the Geppettin species give their speed, tremorsense or disease immunity.

## 0.0.60 (2026-11-18)
- Items whose spells can be cast once each until the next dawn (Instrument of the Bards, Sage's Signet, Idols of Good Fortunes, Necklace of the Beastly Familiar and more) now give one counter per spell, and the cast button stops when that spell is used.

## 0.0.59 (2026-11-18)
- Features that can be refilled by spending a spell slot (Holy Nimbus, Living Legend, Elder Champion, Avenging Angel and about 20 more) now show a Restore button on their card. It spends the lowest slot of the needed level and refills one use.

## 0.0.58 (2026-11-18)
- The Eye and Hand of Vecna now give Poison immunity and Advantage on Initiative. Liquid Courage has a switch for its Frightened immunity.
- Blessings of Knowledge, Student of Preservation, Student of War and Reanimator's Skill Set let you pick an Artisan's Tools proficiency. College of Lore lets you pick three skills.

## 0.0.57 (2026-11-18)
- Five more subclass features now give their spell in the spell list: Street Art, Swindle, Moment To Act, Cup Floweth Over and Faerzress Step.

## 0.0.56 (2026-11-17)
- Seventeen more feats now give the cantrip or spell they teach in the spell list, such as Familiar Friend, Shadow Weaver, Dread Authority and Tadpole Host. Where the feat lets you choose the casting ability, you pick it with the feat.

## 0.0.55 (2026-11-17)
- Darkvision and Blindsight from 12 more subclass features and feats now count on the sheet, such as Eyes of Night, Abyssal Eyes, Impaired Vision and Shadow Pact. Divine Strike of 13 more domains now shows its extra damage die.
- Advantage notes from 10 more features and items now show up, for example Danger Sense of the Dungeoneer and Morose Monocle. Elegant Courtier and Iron Mind now give Wisdom save proficiency.

## 0.0.54 (2026-11-17)
- Fly, Climb and Swim Speeds from about 40 features, items, feats and traits now show on the sheet, with a switch for the ones that only last a while, such as Winged Boots, Wings Unfurled and Elemental Gift.
- Immunities of 16 subclass features now count too. Examples are Hand of Justice, Plague Carrier, Faerzress Form and Avatar of Perfect Order, with a switch where the form is optional.

## 0.0.53 (2026-11-17)
- Blood Mage adds Medicine. Chaplain, Legionary and Master of Fiends now let you pick the skill their bonus proficiency gives.

## 0.0.52 (2026-11-17)
- Seventeen feats now give the cantrip or spell they teach in the spell list, such as Light Bringer, Arcane Artist, Touch of Death and Firebrand. Where the feat lets you choose the casting ability, you pick it with the feat.
