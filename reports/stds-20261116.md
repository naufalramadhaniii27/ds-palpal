# Spells That Don't Suck (Open5e), 0.0.37

Source: open5e/open5e-api, document "Spells That Don't Suck" by Ben Somers (somanyrobots), CC-BY 4.0. Omega Ankh and KibblesTasty credited as in the source.

- 180 spells with full class lists from the Open5e v2 spell data (`srd_*` class ids mapped to class names).
- Converter: `tools/o5_spells.py` writes `tools/tp/o5/stds.json`. `tools/tp/hb.py` resolves `o5/` paths.
- Text: own wording in `tools/tp/overlay/tstds_spell.py` (summary and full text, mechanics kept).
- Book id `tstds`, tag STDS, 2014 rules, off until the player turns it on. Data file `src/data/tp23-tstds.js`.
- 11 names collide with other books and carry the suffix (STDS): Benediction, Eldritch Rift, Force Weapon, Hailstorm, Life Drain, Mind Link, Misdirection, Misfortune, Rain of Fire, Siphon Life, Tempest.
- Tests: `tests/tp_spells.py` gained 87 wizard spells for tstds (on and off, 390x844 and 1250x850). golden_builds 1479, solid_data_contract 627, mech_scan 0 new, split_dist, version_label, guard_all pass.
- Perf budget unchanged: raw 28.56 MB (limit 28.7), gzip 6.37 (6.4), brotli 4.40 (4.42), heap_tools 68.7 (70).
- Not imported: Open5e A5E documents (no spell class lists), Black Flag, Kobold Press `kp` (31 spells, no classes).
