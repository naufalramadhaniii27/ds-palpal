

# Archived What's New entries 0.0.26 to 0.0.31 (moved out of the shell file to keep index.html under 2 MB)

## 0.0.31 (2026-11-13)
- The name you type in a new character now shows on the sheet at once, and the default name is selected so typing replaces it.
- The Equipment step opens much faster: the Add Items catalog starts closed and is drawn only when you open it (it held about 12000 elements).

## 0.0.30 (2026-11-11)
- Psion class (Unearthed Arcana 2025 Psion Update, 2024 rules): Psionic Energy Dice that grow from d6 to d12, Telekinetic Propel, Telepathic Connection, Psionic Restoration, Psionic Surge and Enkindled Life Force, with 11 Psionic Discipline picks at levels 2, 5, 10, 13 and 17.
- Psion subclasses Metamorph, Psi Warper, Psykinetic and Telepath with their always-prepared spells. 10 Wild Talent feats (Atmokinesis, Biokinesis, Clairsentience, Cryokinesis, Empath, Flesh Morpher, Mind Whisperer, Psi Trickster, Psykineticist, Pyrokinesis) with use trackers.
- 17 Psion spells and the Psionic Spirit monster. New classes from third-party books now get their core spell list in the spell step.

## 0.0.29 (2026-11-11)
- Unearthed Arcana 2025 and 2026 (2024 rules): 22 new subclasses (Gladiator, Hell Knight, Circle of the Titan, Demonic Sorcery, Hexblade Patron, Tattooed Warrior, Primordial Patron, Warrior of Venom, Path of Lament, House Agent, Imaskarcanist, Faerzress Sorcery, Freedom Domain and more).
- 24 feats: the Underdark Ceremorphosis path, the Villainous Death Knight and Lich paths, four new Epic Boons and five origin feats. 4 Underdark species (Deep Imaskari, Drider, Illithidkin, Myconid) and the Mind Blast spell.
- The new Unearthed Arcana source is off until you turn it on in Sources.

## 0.0.28 (2026-11-11)
- World of Warcraft (2024 rules): the Death Knight, Demon Hunter, Shaman and Evoker classes are in, each built on the core class it copies (Paladin, Ranger, Barbarian, Sorcerer). Rage, Aura of Protection, Fast Movement and the other core class rules now apply to them.
- 8 subclasses, 12 feats, 69 spells, 13 species with lineage picks and a World of Warcraft Elf, Warlock invocations and Battle Master maneuvers, 64 items (artifacts, potions, mounts, vehicles) and 86 monsters are in.
- Fix: class names with two words (Death Knight, Monster Hunter) are read correctly on a multiclass sheet.

## 0.0.27 (2026-11-11)
- Grim Hollow Campaign Guide (2014 rules): 11 feats, 18 spells including 8 curses, 24 backgrounds with ranks and professions, 9 items and 13 monsters are in.
- Six transformations (Aberrant Horror, Fiend, Seraph, Lich, Vampire, Lycanthrope) with every Boon and Flaw, merged with the Player's Guide boons. Each Level picks a Boon of that or a lower Level. The nine Gifts of Damnation are in.
- Background talents for ten professions, with the Profession Die by rank.

## 0.0.26 (2026-11-11)
- Fix: the Dark Matter (2014 rules) data shipped in 0.0.25 was not loading. It loads now.
- Grim Hollow Player's Guide (2014 rules): 24 subclasses, 42 spells, 40 items, 46 advanced weapons with property texts, 9 species, 10 backgrounds and 7 monsters.
- Transformations (2014 rules): Aberrant Horror, Fey, Fiend, Lich, Lycanthrope, Primordial, Seraph, Specter and Vampire, as one feat per Level with the Boons and Flaws.
- Misfortunes, adventurer's talents and alchemical compounds are pickers on their subclasses.
