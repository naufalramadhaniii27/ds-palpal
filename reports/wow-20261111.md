# World of Warcraft (2024) import notes (0.0.28)

Source: 5etools homebrew "World of Warcraft - 2024". Built by tools/tp_build.py (book twow, file src/data/tp20-twow.js and q06-twow-monster.js).

Decisions
- Death Knight, Demon Hunter, Shaman and Evoker are reskin classes. Each copies the record of its parent (Paladin, Ranger, Barbarian, Sorcerer) through tools/tp_wow.py. The book renames features and swaps a few, listed in the overlays twow_<class>.py.
- Engine: a reskin class now counts under its parent for every rule keyed by the parent name. Changed clsLv (builder), lvOf (sheet), defClass (numbers) and the class label parser. New sheet field clsInh (class name to parent name). A multiclass of Paladin and Death Knight counts both levels as Paladin levels for these rules. Open point.
- Bug fixed on the way: clsLvls on the sheet cut two-word class names (Death Knight became Knight). Fixed in the parser.
- Species: 13 new species and the WoW Elf (key "Elf", shown as Elf (WoW)). Dwarf, Gnome, Human and Orc of the book copy the core 2024 species and are not rebuilt.
- Options: Warlock invocations (Pact of the Legion and six more) and five Battle Master maneuvers join the app lists while the source is on (slot WXO, now merged instead of replaced by the 2014 file).
- Items: 64 items. Mounts and powered vehicles are gear rows. Ashbringer states, Shalamayne halves and Staff of Light and Shadow forms are separate rows.
- Monsters: 86 stat blocks. tp_monsters.py now handles an ability given as a note (Bear Form, Cat Form) and attack text with {@atkr}.

Text only (no counter or picker)
- Soul Fragments grows by +1 at level 13 and +2 at level 17 (counter uses WIS modifier).
- Evoker Spells and Demon Hunter column spells (only the spells shared with Ranger are automatic).
- Weapon Mastery note on a long rest lists only the core class names, so reskin classes do not appear in it.
- Resistance for Draenei, Tauren Taunka lineages is counted. Taunka AC +1 is text.
- Void Warrior feat cantrip pick was not opened in the UI. Data checked only.

Reference only for now (Library phase)
- 33 languages and 35 rewards (charms and blessings), 3 item groups.

Mech scan: 14 WoW features are listed under by_design with a reason in tests/mech_baseline.json. 48 closed gaps were dropped from the list.
