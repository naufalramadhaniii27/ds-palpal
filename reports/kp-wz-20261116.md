# Kobold Press Compilation and Warlock Zine (Open5e), 0.0.39

Sources: open5e/open5e-api documents `kp` (31 spells) and `wz` (43 spells), Kobold Press, OGL 1.0a.

- The v2 rows carry no class lists. The class lists come from the same source in Open5e v1 (`dnd_class`). `tools/o5_spells.py` takes the v1 file as a fourth argument. "Sorceror" is read as Sorcerer. Anti-Paladin has no list in the app and is dropped.
- Imported only spells that are not yet in the app and have at least one class: kp 16 (book tkp, tag KPC), wz 26 (book twz, tag WZ). Own wording in `tools/tp/overlay/tkp_spell.py` and `twz_spell.py`.
- Not imported: kp 15 spells (5 already in the app, 10 with no class in the source), wz 17 spells (already in the app).
- Source text damaged in Doom of Voracity and Summon Clockwork Beast: the app text says so and points to the book.
- Tests: tp_spells (6 kp and 9 wz wizard spells), golden_builds 1479, solid_data_contract 633, mech_scan 0 new, split_dist, version_label, guard_all pass. Raw size 28.62 MB, inside the 28.7 MB budget.
