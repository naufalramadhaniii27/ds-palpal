# Black Flag Reference Document (Mechanist), 2026-11-16

Source: Kobold Press reference document and Open5e v2 data (bfrd). Source id tbf, tag BFR, 2014 rules, own wording.

Built
- Class Mechanist (hit die d8, saves CON and INT, light and medium armor, shields, simple and martial weapons).
- Class table columns Augment Effects Known and Augmented Items.
- 13 Augment effects as picks, counted by the Augment Effects Known column (8 at level 15).
- Heroic Boon pick (Curse of Unmaking or Mend the Broken) at level 10.
- Subclass Metallurgist (Absorbing, Mystic Metal, Heavy Hitter, Juggernaut, Full Metal).
- Trackers: Shard of Creation charges, Rapid Augment uses (INT modifier), Heroic Boon, Absorbing (proficiency bonus), Full Metal.

Not built
- Spellwright subclass: the source data names it but carries no text. Not in the first 500000 characters of the page either.
- Talents (martial and technical) as an alternative to Improvement: the Improvement feature is the standard Ability Score Improvement.
- Theurge, Vanguard, Witch and the other Black Flag classes: not yet imported.

Notes
- The page lists Juggernaut at level 9 in the table and 11 in its heading. Open5e says 11. Built at 11.
