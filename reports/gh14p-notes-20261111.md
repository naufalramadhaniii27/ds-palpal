# Grim Hollow Player's Guide 2014 (GHPG14), notes

- Release 0.0.26 also fixes a miss in 0.0.25: data/tp17-tdm14.js was not listed in src/manifest.json, so the Dark Matter 2014 book data did not load. Now listed with tp18-tgh14p.js and q06-tgh14p-monster.js.
- Source data of Fey-Blessed and Lapsed Inquisitor have their tool, language and gear fields swapped. The printed text wins in tgh14p_bg.py.
- The 2014 transformation data has only Boons and Flaws, with the prerequisite "Nth Level of X Transformation". The Level 1 core features and the Transformation Save rules are not in the data. tp_build.trans_records reads this prerequisite form and names the feats "<X> Transformation: Level N". The pick counts per Level (one Boon at Levels 2 to 4 for Fey, Primordial and Specter) are inferred from the prerequisite chains.
- Advanced weapon properties (ADV_*) are listed in PROP14 and their texts are in tgh14p_item_w.py. Ammunition-only properties (ADV_AAP, ADV_BL, ADV_INC, ADV_SA, ADV_SIL) have no weapon using them.
- Bellows Canister and Paper Cartridge Bullet prices are read as gp from the raw value field.
- mech_baseline.json: 55 new by_design lines for Grim Hollow and now-loaded Dark Matter 2014 rows.
