Art set T4 for our D&D character sheet app. Make exactly 47 images with $imagegen and nothing else.
Rules:
- Each image is 512x512 PNG with real transparency, saved at the path given.
- Do not change any other file in the repo.
- Commit in batches of about 5 images and push each batch to the branch art-t4 (create it from main). Do not open a PR. If you stop early because of limits, report which images are done.
- If an image comes out realistic, 3D or photo-like, regenerate it once before saving.
- At the end, report the image model used and the final pixel size of each file.

Style lock for every image: a classic Studio Ghibli style 2D hand-drawn anime film illustration (original design), cel-shaded with clean thin outlines, flat cel colors with two or three tones, one small highlight, soft top-left light. NOT realistic, NOT a photo, NOT 3D, NOT digital concept art, NOT glossy. Transparent background, subject centered and filling about 75 percent of the canvas, equal margin on all sides, no floor, no cast shadow.
Do not include: any text, letters, numbers, runes that look like writing, logos, watermark, signature, frame, border, UI elements, photorealism, 3D render look, lens flare, chromatic aberration, oversaturated neon, copies of existing film characters or scenes, copies of official D&D artwork.
