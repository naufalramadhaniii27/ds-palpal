## Welcome (intro)
1. Gambar: The Lantern and Quill tavern at evening, warm and busy. Mira and Bram push open the round wooden door and peek in, unsure. Hoot sits on a ceiling beam above the door.
   - Bram: "So this is where people play that dragon game?"
   - Mira: "D&D. I heard you can be anyone."
2. Gambar: Hoot glides down and lands on a table in front of Mira and Bram, one wing raised in greeting. Mira jumps back a little, Bram grins.
   - Hoot: "Welcome, new heroes! I'm Hoot. I teach everyone who walks in here."
   - Bram: "A talking owl. Good start."
3. Gambar: Hoot points a wing at a big round table where Sela sits behind a folding wooden screen with no markings, smiling, with three adult players laughing around her and blank dice on the table.
   - Hoot: "That's Sela. Tonight she runs the game. Learn a little with me, then join her table."
   - Sela: "I'll save you two seats!"
4. Gambar: Close shot of Mira and Bram sitting down at an empty table, Hoot opening its satchel and pulling out a blank twenty-sided die and a blank character card.
   - Hoot: "Two paths: learn how to play, or build your first hero. Pick either one, any time."
   - Mira: "Let's do both!"

## What D&D Is (what-is-dnd)
1. Gambar: Sela sits behind her folding screen with no markings at the round table, hands open as she describes something, Mira and Bram listening with wide eyes; Hoot on the table edge.
   - Hoot: "D&D is a story you tell together. One person is the Dungeon Master, the DM. Everyone else plays one hero each."
2. Gambar: A dream-like view of what Sela describes: a misty mountain pass with a crumbling stone bridge over a gorge and a small cave entrance on the far side, in bright morning colors.
   - Sela: "A crumbling bridge crosses the gorge. On the far side, a cave mouth breathes cold air. What do you do?"
3. Gambar: Back at the table: Bram stands up dramatically with a fist raised, Mira laughing behind her hand.
   - Bram: "I march across the bridge. Fearless!"
   - Hoot: "That's your part: say what your hero does."
4. Gambar: The dream view again: Bram's dwarf self halfway across the crumbling bridge, one stone slipping away beneath his boot, his eyes wide.
   - Sela: "The stones creak. Roll for it, Bram."
   - Hoot: "When the outcome is uncertain, the DM asks for a roll."
5. Gambar: At the table: Bram rolls a blank twenty-sided die across the wood, everyone leaning in to watch it tumble.
   - Hoot: "The dice add surprise. Nobody knows what happens next, not even the DM."
6. Gambar: All five at the table laughing together, Bram wiping his brow in relief, Mira patting his shoulder, Hoot doing a little hop.
   - Hoot: "No one wins or loses D&D. You win by having a great story together."
   - Mira: "I like this game already."

## The d20 Test (d20-test)
1. Gambar: Hoot stands on the table holding up a single blank twenty-sided die with both wings like a treasure, Mira and Bram peering at it.
   - Hoot: "Meet the d20, the twenty-sided die. Almost every risky moment is decided by it."
   - Bram: "Bigger number, better?"
   - Hoot: "Exactly."
2. Gambar: A rooftop scene in a bright town: Mira's adventurer self crouched at the edge of a terracotta roof, eyeing a wide gap to the next roof.
   - Sela: "To leap that gap, make a Strength (Athletics) check. The DC is 15."
3. Gambar: Hoot beside a large blank chalkboard with no writing, pointing at Mira's open hand where a die rests.
   - Hoot: "Roll the d20, then add your modifier. Beat or match the DC and you succeed."
   - Mira: "DC means how hard it is?"
   - Hoot: "Difficulty Class. 10 is easy, 15 is medium, 20 is hard."
4. Gambar: Mira's adventurer self mid-leap across the rooftop gap, coat flying, arms stretched, a pigeon scattering.
   - Hoot: "Three kinds of d20 tests: ability checks for tasks, saving throws to resist danger, and attack rolls to hit."
5. Gambar: A training yard: Bram swings at a straw target dummy wearing a dented helmet, Hoot perched on the dummy's head.
   - Hoot: "For attack rolls, the target number is the enemy's Armor Class, its AC."
   - Bram: "So I need to beat the dummy's armor. Easy."
6. Gambar: Mira landing on the far roof, rolling into a crouch and grinning; Bram cheering from the street below with both arms up.
   - Hoot: "A natural 20 on an attack is a Critical Hit. A natural 1 always misses. For checks, just add and compare."

## Advantage and Disadvantage (advantage)
1. Gambar: A windy seaside cliff: Mira's adventurer self drawing a bow at a distant straw target on a post, a friendly sea breeze behind her.
   - Hoot: "Sometimes luck is on your side. That's Advantage."
2. Gambar: Close shot of Mira's open palm holding two blank twenty-sided dice, Hoot leaning in from the side with a bright look.
   - Hoot: "With Advantage, roll two d20s and keep the higher one."
3. Gambar: Bram on the same cliff in thick fog, squinting with one eye shut, his bow string tangled in a gust.
   - Hoot: "Disadvantage is the opposite: roll two d20s and keep the lower."
   - Bram: "Why does the fog always find me?"
4. Gambar: Hoot balancing on one leg on a wooden post holding a sunbeam in one wing and a wisp of fog in the other, Mira watching.
   - Hoot: "Have both at once? They cancel out. Roll one d20 as normal. Several of each still cancel."
5. Gambar: Sela at the table tossing a small glowing golden token to Bram, who catches it with surprise.
   - Sela: "Brave move, Bram. Take Heroic Inspiration!"
6. Gambar: Bram rerolling a blank die with a huge grin while the golden token fades from his hand, Hoot clapping its wings.
   - Hoot: "Heroic Inspiration lets you reroll any one die right after you roll it. You must keep the new roll, and you can only hold one."

## Abilities and Skills (abilities-skills)
1. Gambar: A sunny village green where six adult adventurers each show off in a playful way: lifting a barrel, balancing on a fence rail, holding a breath under a waterfall, reading a thick book, watching a hawk, charming a grumpy merchant. Hoot stands in front like a teacher.
   - Hoot: "Every hero has six abilities: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma."
2. Gambar: Hoot next to a blank wooden board with six empty round frames, Mira pointing at one, Bram scratching his beard.
   - Hoot: "Each ability has a score, usually 8 to 15 when you start. The modifier is what you add to rolls."
   - Mira: "So a high score gives a bigger bonus."
3. Gambar: Mira's adventurer self sneaking past a dozing guard in a sunny courtyard on tiptoe.
   - Hoot: "Skills are special training under an ability. Sneaking is Stealth, part of Dexterity."
4. Gambar: Bram flexing next to a heavy cart he just pushed out of mud, a farmer cheering.
   - Hoot: "If you're proficient in a skill, add your Proficiency Bonus too. It starts at +2 and grows as you level."
5. Gambar: Mira at the table pointing at Bram's blank character card, both laughing.
   - Mira: "Bram, you're proficient in Athletics but not Stealth?"
   - Bram: "Dwarves don't sneak. We arrive."
6. Gambar: Hoot holding up a small round wooden shield with a calm face while a harmless puff of green mist bounces off it.
   - Hoot: "Saving throws use abilities too. Your class makes you proficient in two of them."

## Your Turn in Combat (combat-turn)
1. Gambar: A forest clearing: a big angry boar-like beast bursting from bushes toward Mira and Bram, who startle; Hoot flapping above holding an hourglass.
   - Hoot: "Combat! First, everyone rolls Initiative, a Dexterity check. Highest goes first."
2. Gambar: Bram stepping forward, measuring his stride with a determined face, a dotted path of footprints behind him.
   - Hoot: "On your turn you can move up to your Speed, often 30 feet. You can split it before and after your action."
3. Gambar: Bram swinging his warhammer at the beast, a clean impact flash, no blood.
   - Hoot: "And you take one action: Attack, Dash, Dodge, Help, Hide, Magic and more."
4. Gambar: Mira tossing a small dagger quickly while also stepping back, a quick light trail behind it.
   - Hoot: "A Bonus Action is extra, but only when a feature or spell says so."
5. Gambar: The beast charging past Mira; she reacts in an instant, catching a branch to swing out of the way.
   - Hoot: "A Reaction happens on someone else's turn, like an Opportunity Attack when a foe moves away. One per round."
6. Gambar: The beast running off into the trees, Mira and Bram catching their breath and grinning at each other, Hoot turning the hourglass over.
   - Hoot: "Then the next creature takes its turn. A round is about six seconds."
   - Bram: "Six seconds felt like a week."

## Hit Points and Rest (hit-points-rests)
1. Gambar: Bram limping slightly with a bruise on his cheek and a torn sleeve, still smiling, Hoot hovering with a small bandage roll. No blood.
   - Hoot: "Hit Points, HP, show how much more you can take. Damage lowers them. Healing raises them, never past your maximum."
2. Gambar: Mira kneeling beside Bram who lies flat on the grass, eyes closed and peaceful, Hoot anxious on his chest. No blood.
   - Hoot: "At 0 HP you fall Unconscious. On each of your turns, make a Death Saving Throw."
3. Gambar: Close shot of a single blank twenty-sided die resting on grass next to Bram's open hand, a faint warm glow.
   - Hoot: "10 or higher is a success. Three successes and you're stable. Three failures and your hero dies. A 20 wakes you with 1 HP."
4. Gambar: Bram sitting up, rubbing his head and laughing, Mira hugging him, Hoot doing a happy flap.
   - Mira: "Never scare me like that again."
   - Bram: "I was just resting my eyes."
5. Gambar: A campsite at dusk: the party sitting by a fire, Bram eating from a bowl, Mira rewrapping a bandage on her arm, Hoot on a branch.
   - Hoot: "A Short Rest is an hour. Spend Hit Point Dice to heal."
6. Gambar: The same camp at night, everyone asleep in bedrolls under stars, the fire low, Hoot keeping watch with one eye open.
   - Hoot: "A Long Rest is 8 hours. You wake with all your HP and Hit Point Dice back, and many abilities refresh."

## Conditions (conditions)
1. Gambar: A sunny training meadow: Hoot standing on a tree stump like a coach, Mira and Bram in practice gear.
   - Hoot: "Conditions change what your hero can do. Here are a few you'll see often."
2. Gambar: Bram knocked flat on his back in the grass by a playful shove, legs in the air, laughing.
   - Hoot: "Prone: you're on the ground. Attacks near you get Advantage, ranged ones get Disadvantage. Standing up costs half your movement."
3. Gambar: Mira held in a bear-hug grip by a big friendly adult half-orc sparring partner, her feet off the ground, both laughing.
   - Hoot: "Grappled: your Speed is 0 until you break free."
4. Gambar: Bram trembling and hiding behind a tree from a harmless big moth flapping at him, Mira giggling.
   - Hoot: "Frightened: Disadvantage on checks and attacks while you can see the source, and you can't move closer to it."
5. Gambar: Mira squinting and coughing through a harmless cloud of flour from a burst sack, white dust everywhere.
   - Hoot: "Poisoned gives Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks. Blinded means you can't see."
6. Gambar: Hoot holding open its satchel full of small blank cards, Mira and Bram dusting themselves off and smiling.
   - Hoot: "No need to memorize them. Tap a condition on your sheet and the app explains it."

## Spells (spells)
1. Gambar: A bright library tower: Sela, now as a wizard, holding an open spellbook with blank pages, three small colored lights circling her hand; Mira watching amazed, Hoot on a bookshelf.
   - Hoot: "Some classes cast spells: wizards, clerics, bards and more."
2. Gambar: Sela flicking a fingertip and a tiny flame dances on a candle wick, casual and easy.
   - Hoot: "Cantrips are small spells you can cast as often as you like."
3. Gambar: Sela opening a small brass lantern holding a bright blue flame, carefully, like spending a coin.
   - Hoot: "Bigger spells use spell slots. Casting one spends a slot of that level or higher. A Long Rest gives them back."
4. Gambar: Sela holding a shimmering soap-bubble-like shield of light around Mira, eyes closed in focus.
   - Hoot: "Some spells need Concentration to keep going. You can concentrate on only one at a time."
5. Gambar: Bram bonks Sela's shoulder with a pillow in a friendly ambush; the light bubble wobbles while she grits her teeth, still focused.
   - Hoot: "Take damage while concentrating? Make a Constitution saving throw, DC 10 or half the damage, whichever is higher. Fail, and the spell ends."
6. Gambar: Mira trying a tiny spark between her own fingers for the first time, delighted, Sela and Hoot cheering.
   - Hoot: "Your sheet tracks your slots and concentration for you. Tap a spell to read what it does."
