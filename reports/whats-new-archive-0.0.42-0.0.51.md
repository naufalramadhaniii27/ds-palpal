
# Archived What's New entries 0.0.42 to 0.0.51 (moved out of the shell file to keep index.html under 2 MB)

## 0.0.51 (2026-11-17)
- Twenty-one species and heritages now list Immunity to disease in Defenses, such as the Chassis forms, the Undead heritage traits and the Mushroomfolk.

## 0.0.50 (2026-11-17)
- Stoneborn now adds +1 AC. Exomorph and Revenant Blade add their AC bonus through a toggle.

## 0.0.49 (2026-11-17)
- The five Feather Robes now add +1 to saving throws. Ingot of the Skold Rune, Orrery of the Wanderer, Knifewing Cape and Jewel of Three Prayers add +1 AC. Blindfold of True Darkness and Helm of Domination add their sense.

## 0.0.48 (2026-11-17)
- Twelve more magic items now list their fixed damage Resistance in Defenses, such as Karrek's Bastion, Talons of the Squall and Warding Brooch.

## 0.0.47 (2026-11-17)
- Twenty-five more magic weapons now show their extra damage die on the weapon rows, such as Flayer Slayer, Dawnbringer and Duskcrusher.

## 0.0.46 (2026-11-17)
- Weapon damage riders now show on the weapon rows for Dragon, Hunger, Lust, Vermin and Zeal Domain (Divine Strike), Bounty Hunter and Freerunner.

## 0.0.45 (2026-11-17)
- Eight magic items now list their Advantage rules in Defenses while carried, such as Gamma Pendant and Ring of Truth Telling.

## 0.0.44 (2026-11-17)
- Defenses now list the Advantage and Disadvantage rules of 40 species, 56 feats and 71 subclasses at the right level, such as Sunlight Sensitivity, Ley-Bound and Fearless.

## 0.0.43 (2026-11-16)
- Fifteen more subclasses now give their fixed tool, armor, weapon and skill training on the sheet, such as Way of the Bow, Mercy Domain and Enforcer.
- Four species and four feats now add their fixed tool or weapon training.

## 0.0.42 (2026-11-16)
- Seventeen subclasses now add their fixed skill proficiencies on the sheet at the right level, such as Hunt Domain, Scout and Arcane Archer.
- Swim, climb and fly speed equal to your Speed now count for seven subclasses and three feats.
